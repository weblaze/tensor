# Demo script (about 12 minutes)

Before: Settings → type RESET → **Reset demo** (dates move to today; Telegram links are kept). Have three screens: laptop (office), supervisor phone linked as Om (`/start OM0142`), doctor phone linked as Dr. Gunjan (`/start GUNJAN`). If procurement should get a message live, link a third phone as Anil (`/start ANIL01`).

## 1. Prashant's screen (Today) — 2 min

- "Needs you today": **Hermetic doors overdue 9 days** (already escalated), **HEPA filters order tomorrow**, **Sanitary fittings in 2 days**, **Bhopal quiet 2 days**, the doctor's OT finish decision waiting 2 days, and site updates waiting for review.
- Point out: nothing healthy is shown in full. Bhopal and Sonipat are cards only because something needs a person.
- Gunjan IVF: "Day 20 / 45 · 3 days behind plan · handover 24 Nov → 27 Nov".
- Click **Remind Rinku** on Bhopal: the supervisor gets a Hindi + English nudge.

## 2. Planner — 3 min

- **New project** → clinic name, city, start date.
- The plan appears from the template. Show:
  - **MGPS pressure test** marked *Learned* — it was added by hand on two past jobs.
  - Lead times marked *Learned*: AHU 28 → 32 days, glass 10 → 13, OT panels 21 → 23–24, hermetic doors 18 → 21, from real past deliveries.
  - **Order by** dates: needed on site minus lead time. With a start 3 days out, three orders are already late — the warning says so.
- Type order notes ("Existing AC to be removed. Cryostorage needs extra power point. Pass box between lab and OT.") → **Suggest from notes** → extra tasks and a material marked *AI*. Untick one to show suggestions are optional.
- **Create project**.

## 3. Supervisor phone — 2 min

- Om sends a photo with "glass panels received but 2 broken, please reorder" (or a Hindi voice note).
- Bot replies: "Logged for Sonipat · Glazing. Office will review." plus Hindi.
- That is all Om ever does.

## 4. Coordinator inbox — 2 min

- The update is on top: note, Glazing, Critical zone, **Issue**, suggested **Procurement + Prashant**, and **Also do: mark Glass partition panels as issue · 2 broken, reorder**.
- **Approve** (or press A). Anil's phone gets the alert with the photo now; Prashant too. The material is marked issue.
- Approve "Phase 1 complete" with Doctor ticked: it goes into tonight's report, not straight to the doctor.

## 5. Doctor phone — 2 min

- Settings → **Run daily checks now** (or wait for 9 PM). The doctor gets "here is today's site report" with a link.
- The report: Day 20 of 45, today's updates with time-stamped photos, filter by trade, coming up next, critical-zone milestones.
- **Needs your decision**: Approve OT wall finish → **Approve**. The coordinator is told.
- **Save as PDF** for their records.

## 6. Autopilot — 1 min

- Inbox side panel: "88% accepted unchanged" over recent reviews.
- "Today it suggests and Abhay confirms. When it is reliably right, Abhay switches on autopilot for confident updates; the rest still wait."
